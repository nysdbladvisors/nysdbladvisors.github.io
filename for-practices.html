What Your Office Is Usually Asked to Do

Most of the paperwork belongs to the patient, not your practice. These are the parts that usually reach a provider.

  • Disability (DBL), including pregnancy: the provider completes Part B, the “Health Care Provider’s Statement,” of the patient’s Form DB-450. For pregnancy, a physician or certified nurse midwife certifies the disability.
  • Paid Family Leave to bond with a new baby: no provider form is required. If the birth certificate is not ready yet, a provider letter confirming the birth is accepted.
  • Paid Family Leave to care for a family member: that family member’s provider completes form PFL-4, after the patient or family member signs a release (form PFL-3).
  • Everything else, including choosing dates, notifying the employer and sending the claim to the insurance carrier, is handled by the patient. The Front Desk Guide has the details.

Rules, forms and amounts change. For the official provider instructions, see paidfamilyleave.ny.gov and the NY Workers’ Compensation Board. Guide last reviewed November 2026.

How This Helps Your Practice

  • Fewer repeated questions. Staff have a one-page answer instead of explaining the rules from scratch.
  • Accurate and plain-language. Figures and form names come from the New York Workers’ Compensation Board and the Paid Family Leave program, and we check them against official sources.
  • Bilingual. Patients can get help in English or Spanish.
  • No work for your office. We never need patient information or records from you, and nothing here asks you to endorse us.

Common Questions From Practices

  • Can we post the flyer or leave copies out? Yes. Print it for your waiting room, exam rooms or checkout desk, or hand it to patients with their paperwork.
  • Does handing this out mean our practice endorses you? No. Handing out information is not an endorsement, and our materials say so. Patients decide for themselves whether to contact us.
  • What if a patient has a question we can’t answer? Point them to the flyer’s QR code or the official State sites. Your staff do not need to explain benefits or eligibility.
  • Do you share patient information with us, or ask for it? No, in either direction.
  • Can we get the flyer with our practice name on it? Email us and we can talk about it.

What We Do and Do Not Do

  • We are an independent advisory service. We are not a law firm, an insurance carrier or a broker, and we are not affiliated with the State of New York.
  • Patients get a free eligibility check and a free 15-minute consultation. Optional paid help with applications is available, and our pricing is listed on our home page. Patients can always apply directly with the State or their insurance carrier at no cost, and are never required to use us.
  • There is no cost to your practice. We do not pay practices for referrals, and we do not accept payment from practices.
  • We do not ask your office for patient information, records or signatures, and we do not contact your patients unless they reach out to us first.
  • Our materials are general information, not medical or legal advice. Patients should confirm details with their doctor and the official sites.
